Common Questions

Frequently asked.

What is included in the cybersecurity audit?
Network architecture review, endpoint security, cloud security audit (AWS/Azure/GCP), access control review, email and phishing defense, data protection analysis, incident response evaluation, third-party risk, and compliance mapping with ISO 27001, SOC 2, IT Act 2000.
How is this different from penetration testing?
Penetration testing simulates attacks to find specific vulnerabilities. Our audit assesses your overall security program—policies, configurations, processes, architecture. Both are valuable; our audit provides strategic context.
Is this aligned with regulatory requirements?
Yes. Our audit framework aligns with ISO 27001, SOC 2, IT Act 2000, RBI cybersecurity guidelines, SEBI requirements, and CERT-In directives. Suitable for regulatory audit preparation.
Do you provide hands-on remediation?
Our audit identifies and prioritizes issues with detailed remediation guidance. Implementation typically requires your IT team or partners. We can recommend implementation partners on request.
What happens after the audit?
You receive a comprehensive report with prioritized findings and remediation roadmap. Many clients use VextIntel Monthly Retainer for ongoing security intelligence and quarterly reassessments.
