Common Questions

Frequently asked.

What is ISO 31000?
ISO 31000 is the international standard for risk management principles and guidelines. It provides a universally recognised framework for identifying, assessing, and managing operational risks.
Who typically requests this assessment?
CFOs and COOs preparing for board reviews, businesses seeking investment or acquisition, companies entering new markets or launching new products, and organisations that have experienced operational failures.
How is this different from an internal audit?
Internal audit verifies compliance with existing controls. Our operational risk assessment identifies risks that may not yet have controls and quantifies exposure using forward-looking scenario analysis.
Is this suitable for fundraising and M&A?
Yes. Investors and acquirers conduct risk due diligence. Our assessment provides the structured risk documentation they expect and demonstrates risk management maturity.
Can you work with our existing risk register?
Yes. We can assess and enhance an existing risk register or build one from scratch. Either approach is scoped at engagement kickoff.
