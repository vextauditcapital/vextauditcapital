Common Questions

Frequently asked.

Is this suitable for board presentations?
Specifically designed for board and audit committee consumption. Format aligns with COSO ERM and ISO 31000 frameworks. Many clients use our reports as the foundation of their quarterly board risk updates.
Can this support M&A or fundraising?
Yes. Acquirers and investors expect rigorous risk visibility. Our assessments support diligence requirements, identify risks before counterparties do. Critical for IPO readiness.
What risk frameworks do you align with?
Aligned with ISO 31000, COSO ERM Integrated Framework, NIST Risk Management Framework, BASEL III (for financial services), and TPRM standards.
How are risks quantified?
Each risk scored on probability (1-5 scale) and impact ($ value or operational severity). Heat maps, risk matrices, and quantified loss expectations included.
Do you handle subsidiaries and international operations?
Yes. Multi-entity, multi-jurisdiction risk assessment is our specialty. Subsidiary-level risk profiles, consolidated enterprise view, cross-border risk dynamics analyzed.
