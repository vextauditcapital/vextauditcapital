Common Questions

Frequently asked.

How many competitors are included?
Standard engagement covers 3 direct competitors in depth. Additional competitors can be added for a supplementary fee. We recommend focusing on your top 3 for the most actionable insights.
What sources do you use?
Public sources including websites, filings, press releases, job postings, social media, review platforms, and industry databases. No proprietary data or unethical intelligence gathering.
How current is the intelligence?
All intelligence is gathered fresh for each engagement. No recycled databases. Current as of the analysis date with market caveats noted where relevant.
Can you cover competitors in specific geographies?
Yes. We cover competitors globally including India, UAE, UK, Singapore, USA, and other markets. Geographic scope specified at engagement kickoff.
Is this useful for fundraising?
Yes. Investors expect founders to have clear competitive intelligence. Our analysis provides the structured competitive landscape data that investor presentations and due diligence require.
