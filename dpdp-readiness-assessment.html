Common Questions

Frequently asked.

What are the penalties under DPDP Act 2023?
Penalties include up to ₹250 crore for failure to prevent breaches, ₹200 crore for children's data violations, ₹150 crore for failing data principal rights, and ₹50 crore for other violations. Penalties are imposed per violation.
Does DPDP apply to my business?
If you process personal data of any Indian citizen or resident, DPDP applies. This includes employee data, customer data, vendor information, and website visitor data. There are no exemptions based on company size, revenue, or industry.
What is a Significant Data Fiduciary?
Companies designated as Significant Data Fiduciaries must appoint a DPO, conduct independent audits, and complete Data Protection Impact Assessments. Designation depends on volume of data, sensitivity, and risk level.
How is DPDP different from GDPR?
DPDP is specific to India's legal context with different penalty structures, narrower territorial scope, and unique features like consent managers and data protection board structure. GDPR compliance does not equal DPDP compliance.
What documentation will I receive?
You receive the complete assessment report, DPDP-compliant notice templates, consent forms, internal policies, breach response procedures, DPO evaluation, penalty exposure analysis, and prioritized 90-day implementation roadmap.
