Common Questions

Frequently asked.

What FEMA penalties can exporters face?
FEMA violations attract penalties up to 3X the transaction value. Late realization of export proceeds, non-repatriation, and unreported transactions are common violations. Our audit identifies these before RBI does.
Which export schemes do you cover?
We cover RoDTEP, SEIS, MEIS, EPCG, Advance Authorization, Duty Drawback, IGST Export Refunds, SEZ benefits, Status Holder benefits, Deemed Exports, and other DGFT schemes.
How much can I typically recover?
Most exporters have ₹1-5 lakh in unclaimed or missed benefits. Some clients have recovered ₹15-30 lakh in legacy claims. The audit fee is typically recovered within the first month.
Do you handle services exporters too?
Yes. We extensively cover IT/ITES exports, SaaS, consulting services, and other service exporters. SEIS scheme, software export FEMA compliance, and OFAC/sanctions screening are particularly relevant.
What about EDPMS and BRC compliance?
Complete EDPMS reconciliation, BRC issuance verification, settlement period adherence, and outstanding receivables analysis included. Critical for FEMA compliance and tax audit preparation.
