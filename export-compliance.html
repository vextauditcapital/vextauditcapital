Common Questions

Frequently asked.

What FEMA penalties can exporters face?
FEMA violations attract penalties up to 3X the transaction value. Late realization of export proceeds, non-repatriation, and unreported transactions are common violations.
Which export schemes do you cover?
RoDTEP, SEIS, MEIS, EPCG, Advance Authorization, Duty Drawback, IGST Export Refunds, SEZ benefits, Status Holder benefits, and other DGFT schemes.
Do you handle services exporters too?
Yes. IT/ITES exports, SaaS, consulting services, and other service exporters extensively covered. SEIS scheme and software export FEMA compliance especially relevant.
What about EDPMS and BRC compliance?
Complete EDPMS reconciliation, BRC issuance verification, settlement period adherence, and outstanding receivables analysis included.
