Common Questions

Frequently asked.

Who needs a GST audit?
Any business with annual turnover of ₹5 crore or above benefits most. Mandatory for businesses above ₹2 crore turnover. Especially valuable for multi-state operations, e-commerce sellers, exporters, and manufacturers handling complex ITC scenarios.
How is AI-powered audit different from traditional consultants?
Traditional consultants typically miss 30-40% of compliance issues due to manual review limitations. Our AI analyses every transaction, every return, and every input claim delivering comprehensive insights that would take weeks manually.
Is my data secure?
All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We follow ISO 27001 protocols and DPDP Act compliance. Data is automatically deleted 30 days after audit completion unless you request extended retention.
Does this replace my CA or tax consultant?
No. We complement your existing tax team by providing an independent, AI-powered review. Our findings give your tax team a comprehensive starting point with regulatory citations and evidence.
What if I disagree with the findings?
Every finding includes supporting evidence, regulatory references, and confidence scores. If you disagree, our experts will review your perspective and either adjust the finding or explain the rationale in detail.
